# Privacy Policy

My Little Chef · Effective date: November 24, 2026

**Summary.** My Little Chef, offered by Summit Wanderlust, LLC, uses your information to run your account, show what you post, and connect to Yammoing if you ask it to. It has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell personal information or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account.** You sign in with Apple or Google. Firebase Authentication keeps the email address (Apple's relay address if you hide yours), the name and any profile photo link your provider gives us, sign-in details and a user ID. We don't show your email to other people.

**Profile.** Your display name, username, bio, and follower and following counts. Signed-in users can see your profile. We also store when you accepted the posting terms.

**What you post and do.** When you post a recipe, we store its text, details and, if it has one, a cover picture web link. Posts are visible to everyone signed in. We store who you follow, which other signed-in users may be able to see, and the recipes you like. Your likes and block list are private to you, though a recipe shows its total likes.

**Reports.** If you report a recipe or profile, we store the target, reason, any details you write, the time and your user ID.

**Yammoing link.** If you link Yammoing, we store both account IDs, a shared ID, when you linked, and the times of recent sends (to limit how many you can make). Every account gets this ID at first sign-in, so it can be linked later.

**Technical data.** Firebase and Google Cloud process your IP address, request details, user ID and App Check device attestation to run and protect the service. Our server logs record your user ID when an account is deleted or a request fails.

## 2. Information that stays on your device

Unless you post it, your own recipes, saved recipes, notes, cooking history, drafts, dietary preferences, ingredients and settings stay on your device. We don't have a copy. (Your device's own backups may include them.)

- **Photos:** photos you take or choose stay on your device, and dish recognition runs there. A photo is not uploaded when you post.
- **Voice:** cook mode reads steps aloud with the iPhone's built-in voices. The app doesn't use the microphone.
- **Timers:** notifications are scheduled on your device, if you allow them.

You can change camera, photo and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

## 3. How we use information

To sign you in, run your profile and posts, connect to Yammoing at your request, review reports, prevent abuse, fix problems and meet legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Who receives it

- **Other signed-in users** see your profile and posted recipes.
- **Google Firebase and Google Cloud** host accounts, the database, recipe pictures, server code and logs.
- **Apple and Google** handle sign-in.
- **TheMealDB** receives the dish, ingredient or category the app looks up, including a dish recognised from a photo, and your IP address.
- **Picture hosts.** Some recipe pictures load from other websites, including links people add to posts. Those sites, which currently include archanaskitchen.com, see your IP address.
- **Websites you open**, such as a recipe's source, follow their own policies.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect people or the service, or in a business transfer with appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

### Yammoing, if you link it

Yammoing is another app from Summit Wanderlust, LLC. Nothing is shared until you approve the link in Yammoing. While linked:

- **My Little Chef can see** your Yammoing shopping list (item ID, name, quantity and note) and the items in your fridge, freezer and pantry (ID, name, amount, place, how and when added, and picture link). Our server passes them to your app and does not store them.
- **You can send Yammoing** recipe ingredients (recipe name, item names and amounts) and meals you cooked (recipe name, servings, ingredients and amounts). We pass these on and do not keep them.
- **The apps exchange** the account IDs and shared ID needed to keep the link.

You can disconnect in Settings, or in Yammoing under Settings > Linked apps. That stops sharing but does not remove what Yammoing already received, which is covered by [Yammoing's Privacy Policy](https://yammoing.com/privacy).

## 5. Keeping and deleting

We keep account data while your account exists. **My Kitchen > Delete Account** permanently deletes your profile, username, posts, likes, follows, blocks, link and sign-in record. It also tells Yammoing the link has ended; your Yammoing account is not deleted.

- Reports you filed are kept without your identity, including the details you wrote, so we can finish reviewing them. Reports about you are deleted.
- Copies of your recipe that other people made may remain, with the ID of your deleted recipe but not your profile.
- Server logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted automatically.
- Deleting your account does not erase recipes and settings on your device. Delete the app to remove those.

Without a way into the app, you can ask us to delete your account by email.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, and to object to or restrict its use. California residents have rights under the CCPA; we do not sell or share personal information as that law defines it, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights. For people in the EEA and UK, our legal bases are performing our contract with you, legitimate interests (safety and security), consent and legal obligations. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

## 7. Children

My Little Chef is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you think a child has given us information.

## 8. Security and location

Information is processed in the United States and where our providers operate. We use authentication, App Check, database access rules and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure.

## 9. Changes and contact

We'll update the effective date when this policy changes, and tell you in the app or another suitable way if the change is material.
