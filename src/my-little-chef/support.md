# My Little Chef Support

## Contact us

[contact@summitwanderlust.com](mailto:contact@summitwanderlust.com)

Tell us what you were doing and your iPhone and iOS version. Please don't send passwords.

## Report a recipe or person

- **Recipe:** open it, open the menu and tap **Report recipe**.
- **Profile:** open it, tap the **…** menu and tap **Report**.
- **Block:** use **Block** in the same menus. Unblock people in **Settings > Blocked accounts**.

We review reports within 24 hours. You can also email us. If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services.

## Post or remove a recipe

Open the recipe's menu and tap **Post**. Posted recipes are public to everyone signed in. Tap **Unpost** to take yours down.

## Cook mode timers

Allow notifications in iOS Settings > Notifications > My Little Chef. Otherwise timers only sound while the app is open.

## Connect Yammoing

1. In **My Kitchen**, tap the gear, then **Connect** under Yammoing Connection.
2. Approve in Yammoing. If it doesn't open, enter the code in Yammoing under **Settings > Linked apps**.

You need an up-to-date Yammoing app. To unlink, tap **Disconnect** in the same place.

Once linked, you can add a recipe's ingredients to your Yammoing list, see your Yammoing food in **Create > My Ingredients**, and log a cooked recipe. Meals are saved only after you review them in Yammoing.

## My recipes on another iPhone

Your own recipes, saved recipes, notes and cooking history stay on your device. They don't sync with your account.

## Delete your account

Go to **My Kitchen**, tap **Delete Account**, and confirm. This permanently removes your account and everything you've posted, and unlinks Yammoing. It can't be undone. Your Yammoing account isn't affected. Recipes saved on your iPhone stay until you delete the app.

If you can't get into the app, email us to ask for deletion.
